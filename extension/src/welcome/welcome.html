Crunchyroll

You're ready to go

Dual subtitles are already turned on. There is nothing to set up.

  1. It already works

    Open any episode on Crunchyroll. Within a few seconds the original subtitle and its translation appear over the video, as two lines. No API key, no account.

    Is Crunchyroll already open in another tab? Reload it once.

  2. The toolbar icon is the settings

    The icon sits in the browser's toolbar, to the right of the address bar. Click it to change the two languages, the look of the lines and the translation service.

    If you cannot see it, click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin this extension. An unpinned icon is the most common reason people think an extension is not working.

  3. Keep the words you learn

    Hover a word in the original line to see its meanings. Click it and the word is filed in your notebook, together with the sentence and the episode it came from.

    The notebook exports as an Anki deck, CSV or JSON.
